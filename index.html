strategy and decison analysis on hktv mall

This project outlines the corporate culture, historical background, and strategic analysis of HKTVmall. discussion on why university education becomes unpopular

This project the importance for different level of people.At the individual level, universities act as foundational starting points for specialized industries, structured knowledge acquisition, professional training, and lifelong networking among future elites. At the societal level, higher education institutions function as academic repositories, dynamic cultural melting pots, and vital social elevators. They cultivate industry talent, foster cutting-edge ideas, promote democratic citizenship, and enable upward socioeconomic mobility for individuals from diverse backgrounds, helping nations and enterprises navigate modern professional and cultural demands.
